# Ernesto Tages Website - User Guide

## Website Overview

**Purpose:** This professional website showcases Ernesto Tages' consultancy services in ethical leadership, providing information about individual consultancy, professional conferences, resources, and contact options.

**Access:** Public website, accessible to all visitors.

---

## Powered by Manus

Your website is built with cutting-edge web technologies and deployed on enterprise-grade infrastructure:

**Frontend:** React 19 with TypeScript provides a modern, type-safe foundation. Tailwind CSS 4 enables rapid, responsive styling. shadcn/ui components deliver accessible, polished interactions. Wouter handles client-side routing for seamless navigation.

**Deployment:** Auto-scaling infrastructure with global CDN ensures fast loading times worldwide, automatic HTTPS security, and 99.9% uptime reliability.

## Using Your Website

Your website features five main pages accessible through the top navigation menu:

**Home Page** introduces visitors to your core message with the headline "Turning Leadership Dilemmas into Decisive, Ethical Action." Click "Discover how I can help" to navigate to Services, or scroll down to read about strategic support for critical decisions.

**About Me** presents your biography and approach philosophy. Visitors can read your professional background and click "Learn more about my approach" to scroll to the detailed philosophy section. The "Explore my programs" button directs to Services.

**Services** outlines your two work lines with professional photographs: Individual Consultancy (showing a professional meeting) and Professional Conferences (showing a conference presentation). Click on each section to expand detailed information about objectives, modalities, and expected outcomes. Click "Request an exploratory dialogue" or "Request a proposal for your organization" to open the Contact page.

**Resources** offers downloadable insights and frameworks. Visitors can click "PDF download" buttons to access essays like "The Inner Compass of the Ethical Leader" (currently showing "coming soon" notifications). The newsletter signup form at the bottom collects email addresses.

**Contact** provides a simple form with "Name," "Email," and "Message" fields. Click "Send message" to submit inquiries (currently shows a success notification without backend processing).

## Managing Your Website

Access the Management UI panel on the right side of your screen to control website settings and content:

**Preview Panel:** View your live website as visitors see it. Test all navigation links and forms to ensure everything works correctly.

**Code Panel:** Browse all website files and download the complete codebase. Use this to share files with developers or make backups.

**Settings Panel:**
- **General:** Update website name and logo by modifying VITE_APP_TITLE and VITE_APP_LOGO environment variables.
- **Domains:** Customize your auto-generated domain prefix (xxx.manus.space) or connect a custom domain you own.

**Dashboard Panel:** Monitor website traffic with UV/PV analytics after publishing. Control visibility settings to make your site public or private.
